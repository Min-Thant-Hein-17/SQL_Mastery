ANSWER THE FOLLOWING QUESTIONS IN 1-2 Sentences. (3 marks each)

1. Explain the relationship between owner and property for rent. (1-2 sentences)

2. Is it possible for an individual to hold ownership without being classified as either a private or business owner? Why?

3.  Is it possible for an individual to hold both private and business ownership at the same time? Why?


Answer: 

1. Owner can have one or many properties for rent. Thus, one to many relationship. But every single property is owned by only one owner. Thus, one to one relationship. 

 

2. No, it is not possible for an individual to hold ownership without being classified as either a private or business owner, because the diagram shows "Mandatory, Or" specialization, which means each owner must be either a PrivateOwner or a BusinessOwner.

 

3. No, it is not possible for an individual to hold both private and business ownership at the same time, because the specialization is "OR". Therefore, an owner can only belong to one subtype at a time.
